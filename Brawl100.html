P1: A/D move, W jump, S fast-fall/drop, F attack, G special, H dash (i-frames), R ULT, T counter | P2: arrows, "," attack, "." special, "/" dash, ";" ULT, ' counter
Counter: press just BEFORE a foe ults to freeze them and spam attack for a 4-hit finisher, or press when comboed 3+ times to break out with a shockwave. Attack + direction (within ~0.1s): up = uppercut, side = smash, down = spike/sweep; falling + attack = slam; attack mid-dash = dash slash. Esc pauses, 4 opens the main menu.
PARRY: tap dash right as a hit lands (2-frame window). Dash also escapes hitstun. Ult: time freezes and everyone glows; move keys pick the target, attack confirms. Area ults deal 1.5x to the target.
